# HW3 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

Across all five companies, executive changes mostly came before earnings announcements: 22 of the 30 events were filed before the nearest earnings filing, 6 after, and 2 in the same week. Several filings reported multiple changes at once (Walmart's January 16, 2026 filing alone produced five rows), so counting unique filings gives a less lopsided but similar picture: 11 before, 4 after, and 1 in the same week. The pattern varies by company: JPMorgan and Walmart had no events after earnings, Apple and NVIDIA leaned toward before, and Microsoft was the only company whose changes mostly came after earnings, all of them board-level changes. One possible explanation is that companies prefer to announce leadership changes ahead of earnings so the news is absorbed before results are discussed, but with only 16 filings over one year and some extraction errors in names and titles, this is a pattern worth watching rather than a firm conclusion.
